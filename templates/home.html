HRV based Stress Detection Using ANN

Detect stress states from heart-rate variability features.

This web app runs your trained ANN locally and predicts one of three study-relevant conditions: Medium level stress, Low stress, or High stress.

Start Prediction Project Overview
Time-domain HRV (e.g., RMSSD, SDRR) Frequency-domain HRV (LF/HF, TP) ANN classifier (Keras) StandardScaler

Fast start

Head to Prediction, fill feature values (or generate random values), and get an instant class output.

Medium level stress Low stress High stress

What the model sees

The input is a vector of HRV-derived features (time + frequency domain), scaled using the same StandardScaler used during training.

  • Time domain: MEAN_RR, MEDIAN_RR, SDRR, RMSSD, SDSD, pNN25, pNN50
  • Frequency domain: VLF/LF/HF, TP, LF_HF, HF_LF
  • Nonlinear: SD1, SD2, sampen, higuci (as included in your dataset)

Why these classes matter

Your notebook uses condition labels mapped to: Medium level stress, Low stress, and High stress. These align well with study scenarios where workload and interruptions shift autonomic balance.
